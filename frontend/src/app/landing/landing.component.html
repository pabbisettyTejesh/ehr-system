How it works, by role

Three portals, one shared record, access controlled at every step.

Patient

Your record travels with you

  • Get a unique Patient UID the moment you register
  • View your full medical timeline, prescriptions & allergies
  • See exactly who accessed your data, and why — including emergencies
  • Your UID alone never exposes clinical detail to anyone unappointed
Doctor

Full access, only when appointed

  • Registration requires admin approval before you can log in
  • Full record access only for patients linked to you via an appointment
  • Log encounters, diagnoses, prescriptions & allergy updates
  • Emergency mode: critical-only data by Patient UID, reason mandatory, always logged
Admin

Keeps the access model honest

  • Approves or rejects every doctor before they can practice on the platform
  • Creates the appointment that becomes a doctor's access link to a patient
  • Can register patients directly, without touching clinical data
  • Full visibility into access logs and emergency access logs
